The Power Platform roadmap is moving into the AI at Work roadmap

September 2026

Starting in September 2026, Microsoft is moving the roadmap content for Dynamics 365, Power Platform and Dataverse to the AI at Work roadmap — a single destination for Microsoft 365, Copilot, agents, and Business Applications roadmap updates.

What this means in practice: one place to track what is coming across the whole Microsoft business stack, instead of separate roadmap pages per product line.

For Power Platform folks specifically, this matters because release planning and disclosure now lives under the same umbrella as Copilot and Microsoft 365. If you follow roadmap items for Power Apps, Power Automate or Dataverse, the new home is the AI at Work roadmap.

The existing release plans remain available for historical reference for now.

Source: Partner Center announcements — September 2026 (Microsoft Learn) and Power Apps 2026 release wave 1 planned features.
